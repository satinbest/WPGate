## 🏛️ Attribution & Heritage

WPGate originated as an architectural refactoring and modernization of the **Ghateino** plugin concept, originally authored by **Matin Shokri and the Shokrino Team** (https://shokrino.com).

WPGate completely re-engineers the original implementation into a clean, standalone, object-oriented module, resolving critical security concerns, eliminating runtime disk mutations, removing unauthorized third-party theme references, and adding dual deployment loaders and automated test suites. We acknowledge and respect the original creator's vision for improving site resilience during internet disruptions.
